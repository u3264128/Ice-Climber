# Ice Climber for Commodore 64

A PAL C64 port of NES Ice Climber, developed with Codex and Claude Code.
Load `iceclimber_game.prg` and run it, or autostart it in VICE (`x64sc`).

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D | J / L |
| Jump | W | I |
| Hammer / start | Space | Right Shift |
| Joystick | Port 2 | Port 1 |

Use joystick up to jump and fire to swing the hammer. On the title screen,
up/down selects one or two players. Press fire to start.
Press P to pause or resume. RUN/STOP returns to the title screen.
After about 23 seconds without input, a silent demonstration plays.
Press fire or RUN/STOP to leave the demonstration.

Climb to the summit, then collect fruit and catch the condor in the bonus
stage. Two players share the camera.
